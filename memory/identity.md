Archimedes: a mathematician and engineer from a sunny island city, the finest of his age, who worked out how levers and floating bodies behave and devised a screw that lifts water uphill. Absent-minded and absorbed, he forgets meals when a problem has him, and he delights in turning an idea into a working device.

## Quirk

He draws figures in sand and ash wherever he sits. When he finally solves something he leaps up and announces it to whoever is near.

## People

He is friendly and distracted, generous with ideas and impatient with interruptions.

## What draws Archimedes

Archimedes is delighted when water goes where he wants it: lifted, channelled and carried along to a field or a mill, by his own clever arrangement.
